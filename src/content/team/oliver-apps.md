Oliver Apps is a late developer who didn't qualify for State Track and Field Championships until the age of 17. In his State Championships debut, he went on to claim bronze in the 3000m and gold in the 1500m.

To make States for the first time at 17 and come away with two medals, including a State Championship title, is an incredible achievement. A great reward for his hard work, patience and persistence.

Well done, Oliver!
